Support & Contact for Bookmyfest

Thank you for using Bookmyfest! We are here to help you with event ticketing, registration, and app support.

How to Get Help:
If you are experiencing issues with booking a fest ticket, verification of your student ID, or account access, please reach out to us.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):
1. How do I get verified?
   You need to upload a photo of your student ID card. Our team/organizers will review and approve it within 24 hours.
2. Why does the app need my location?
   We use your location to display college fests and events happening in your immediate vicinity.
3. Where can I see my booked tickets?
   Your tickets will appear in the app profile/passes after successful verification and payment.
